# Shared Language Rule

The normative language policy is the project's root `AGENTS.md` under
`## Conventions` (the shipped skeleton defaults to English for reasoning, code,
and technical documents, and Japanese with the user).
`.claude/rules/orchestration.md` `## Language Protocol` points there.

Every runtime must apply that shared policy to its conversation, agents,
skills, hooks, and generated documents. This rule does not override or
duplicate the shared contract.
